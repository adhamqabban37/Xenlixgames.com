Pulse Paths

How to play

Simple rules, clever chains

You win when every node is lit and no arrows are left on the board.

Arrows

Tap an arrow to fire it. Its pulse travels in a straight line until it leaves the board or hits something.

Nodes

A node lights up when a pulse of its own colour reaches it. Once lit, it lets any pulse pass through.

Switches & gates

Gates only let pulses through in one direction. Hit a switch, or light a linked node, and its gates rotate.

Locks & walls

A lock opens once every node of its colour is lit. Walls never move.

Features

Made to be fair and relaxing

Three stars per level

Solve a level in the minimum number of moves (par) for three stars. Every level is solver-proven, so par is always possible.

Undo and hints

Undo any move, even after a loss, or ask for a hint that shows the next move of a shortest solution.

A gentle start

A 10-level tutorial teaches one idea at a time, then 30 Circuits levels put them together.

No ads, no tracking

No internet connection needed, no accounts and no personal data collected. Privacy Policy.
